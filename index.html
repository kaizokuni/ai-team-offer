Questions owners ask

Isn't this just a $15 chatbot?

A $15 tool runs in someone else's cloud and gives generic answers. This team is installed on your computer, checks your real Shopify orders before answering, and asks you before anything is sent.

I'm not technical. Can I really use this?

That's who it's built for. You chat with your team on Discord, from your phone or your PC. There is no dashboard to learn, and we set everything up with you.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

Never. You create each access code yourself, it stays on your computer, and you can cut it in one click.

What if it sends something wrong to a customer?

It can't send anything on its own. Every reply, e-mail and ad waits for your yes.

Could my Facebook ad account get banned because of this?

The ads agent only prepares paused ads in your existing account. Nothing is published or paid until you launch it yourself.

Where does my data go?

The team runs on your computer and uses AI services in your own name (Claude, ChatGPT and the others listed above). Nothing passes through us or through a server of ours.

I already have someone for customer service.

Then the agent takes the repetitive questions, and your person keeps the cases that need a human.

Can I take only one or two agents?

It's one package because the agents share your store's information and the same approval system. That's what makes them useful together.

What happens if I stop the €100 a month?

You keep everything that is installed. Fixes, updates and the included hours stop; later changes are €25 an hour if you need them.

Why trust one person rather than an agency?

You see and approve everything live, one person is responsible for all of it, and nothing happens without your yes. That's more control than most agencies give you.
